<Closer>
The teams getting the most out of agents in 2026 aren't picking one
model. The pattern that works: route hard reasoning to Opus 4.7,
bulk tool calls to Sonnet 4.6 or GPT-5.4, RAG-over-corpus to Gemini,
and long-running orchestration to Kimi K2.6. Single-model
deployments leave a meaningful chunk of the available quality on
the table — how big a chunk depends entirely on how mixed your
workload is, and you'll only know once you've measured.
</Closer>
